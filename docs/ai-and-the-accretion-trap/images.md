1. **The eyeroll (hero candidate).** A 1960s telephone engineer in shirtsleeves, standing in a vast hall of relay racks and switchboards, eyebrows raised and eyes rolling, while a young man in a narrow tie holds up a diagram of a mesh network with no centre; black-and-white engraving in the style of a 1920s technical magazine, fine hatching, high contrast, no text.
2. **The scaffold that became the building.** A cathedral built around a single small, crooked stone, the stone almost hidden by an immense lattice of wooden scaffolding that has grown so dense it has become the building itself, with tiny workers still adding poles at the top; a 16th-century woodcut, bold black lines on cream paper, no text.
3. **Stoll's mall.** A 1990s shopping mall food court, crowded and bright, seen through the window of a quiet room where one beige computer with a blinking cursor sits on a desk; a cross-hatched pen drawing in the manner of a New Yorker cartoon, no text.
4. **The parrot with a compiler.** A grey parrot perched on a lectern reading from a book, while three stern clerks at a long table behind it check each word against ledgers and stamp a seal on the ones that pass; Renaissance woodcut, no text.
5. **Eating the column.** A bearded man in a suit on a conference stage, solemnly eating a newspaper page out of a bowl with a spoon, while an audience of engineers applauds; a line engraving in the style of a 19th-century illustrated weekly, no text.
6. **Load-bearing scaffolding.** A medieval bridge whose original arch has crumbled away entirely, the traffic now carried by the dense wooden scaffolding that was put up to repair it, nobody noticing; woodcut, no text.
7. **The surfers.** A long reading room where dozens of librarians in 1990s office clothes sort an endless conveyor belt of web pages into wooden card-catalogue drawers, the belt vanishing out of the window into the sky; engraving, no text.
